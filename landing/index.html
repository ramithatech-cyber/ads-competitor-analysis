FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Do I need to connect my ad account?

No. Spyra only needs the ad itself: copy, image, video or a link. Competitor data comes from the public Facebook Ads Library.

How does Spyra decide who my competitors are?

It reads what each advertiser's own ads sell and keeps only those selling the same thing to the same buyer, with a quote from their ad as proof. Noise like unrelated brands or job posts is left out.

Are the buyer reactions real people?

No. They're AI personas matched to your audience. They're a fast signal for comparing ads, and they're clearly labelled as simulated in every report.

How long does an analysis take?

Usually a few minutes, depending on how many competitors are running ads and whether there are videos to watch.

Can I try it before signing up?

Yes. Hit "Try the demo" to use a shared demo account straight away, then create your own free account when you're ready.
