How it works

From one ad to a battle plan in minutes

No setup, no pixel, no ad account access. Drop in the ad you're about to run and Spyra does the rest.

Paste the copy, upload the image or video, or share a link. Spyra reads the hook, offer, visuals and voiceover.

Find real competitors

We scan the Facebook Ads Library and keep only advertisers selling what you sell, each backed by a quote from their own ad.

Run the scroll test

Your ad and theirs go into one feed. 30 AI buyers matched to your audience scroll it and tell you who stopped them, and why.

Get your playbook
